### About Me

I'm an AI & Data Science engineer — State Engineering Diploma + Master's, **ENSIA**, *Mention Très Bien* (2026) — working on multimodal machine learning, retrieval-augmented generation, and secure AI systems for sensitive institutional environments.

- 🔭 Currently building production **RAG assistants** and **RPA compliance engines** at **Nouara**, operating across Algerian government databases (CNRC/DGI) on zero-data-leakage, credential-vaulted architectures.
- 🎓 My final-year thesis — *"Multimodal Sentiment Analysis and Emotion Recognition in Conversations"* (with the University of Oulu, Finland) — tackled **"acoustic drowning"**: multimodal models that are handed audio, video, and text but quietly learn to just read the transcript.
  - Reliability-aware fusion architecture (**HRX + MRE + SAG**) → **87.98%** accuracy on CMU-MOSEI
  - Transfer-aware ensemble for emotion recognition → **71.56%** weighted F1 on MELD, surpassing SPCL & InstructERC
  - Found that **79.3%** of free-form generative-ERC outputs were parser artifacts inflating reported metrics — established a strict-token evaluation protocol as a fix
- 🏭 Past experience: industrial telemetry / SCADA-DCS at **Sonatrach**, telecom-scale behavioral analytics at **Djezzy** (DSIP3 program, with MajestEYE & Huawei)
- 🌱 Interested in: multimodal ML, GenAI infrastructure, applied NLP, secure & auditable AI systems
- 💬 Ask me about: RAG pipelines, multimodal fusion, LLM evaluation methodology, RPA automation
